Moving Room Templates

The dimensions and geometry of every printed piece, and the template kit to generate or paint onto.

The idea

One kit, three scenes, two layers

Every scene hangs the same six printed pieces, in the same sizes, on its own pipes. So a single set of templates serves the parlor, the forest and the kingdom; only the pictures differ. Each scene is drawn as two layers: a drop layer, 50 × 25 ft, that becomes the printed backdrop, and a portal layer, 62 × 25 ft, that holds the canopy border and both leg zones with the opening left empty. Paint or generate onto the layers, stack them to preview, then run the slicer to get the six Gladsbuy files, the drop included.

Inside a leg

The leg template is one design zone 12 ft wide. Its onstage foot (19 to 20 ft from centre) is the strip the centre seats see peeking inside the portal leg, so the pattern must run continuously across the portal-leg edge line. The top 10 ft hang behind the canopy: let the pattern continue but spend no detail there. The eye level runs at 4 ft, the bottom 2 ft go to plum ground, and the last 6 in are the pocket.

From the house the printed legs are narrow. Centre seats see the Snow portal leg from 20 to 23.5 ft off centre, the Sweets portal leg from 20 to 22.5 and the parlor portal leg from 20 to 21, the rest sitting behind the black legs downstage of them. Side seats see much more, out to about 24 ft for Snow and 29 to 30 ft for Sweets and the parlor, so the whole leg gets finished but the outer three feet can be simpler.

Leg template, house left. Raspberry dashes: the portal-leg edge. Grey dashes: the Gladsbuy seam.

Where the blacks went. Moving the snow and sugar legs to the halfway pipes displaced two black pairs: Legs #3 now hangs on LS8 at 8 ft with a 45-ft opening, and Legs #4 on LS15 at 16.7 ft with a 42-ft opening, in every cue. Legs #4 is the mid-depth masker that closes the side seats for the parlor, and 42 is as wide as it can go before the widest seat sees past the parlor portal’s offstage edge. No black leg has to fly during the show, and every printed leg pair has a black pair close behind it to stop light bleed. The model audits clean in all six cues, and its pipe table now lets you drag any piece to another pipe, shifting the ones between like layers, with the electrics and the traveler pinned.

The arch

The canopy cut line

The arch is the maximum opening: the illustrated silhouette may hang below it, never rise above it. It is the model’s curve, an ellipse that meets the legs vertically, 16 ft above the deck at the legs and 20 ft at centre.

Rules along the edge

  • Swag zone. Scallops, tassels and drips may hang up to 18 in below the arch line. The fabric ends at 15 ft, so nothing can hang lower than that.
  • Modules. A 14-ft centre motif (±7 ft) and four 12-ft units (7 to 19 ft and 19 to 31 ft each side). Unit boundaries fall on the template’s gold dashes.
  • Silhouette. Every shape that becomes a cut edge is at least 8 in wide, and the slicer prints a 1-in plum band just inside the edge. Draw the plum shadow line into the art as well.
  • Seen from below. The eye is at 4 ft and the canopy hangs at 15 to 25 ft, so draw its underside: soffits, the insides of swags, the bottoms of boughs.

  1. Generate or paint by unit, not by scene. The drop is a 2:1 image. The canopy is a 7:5 centre motif and a 6:5 swag unit repeated four times, mirrored across centre. The leg is a 1:2 image cropped to 12:25. Make every unit at 2,000 px wide or more and upscale twice; that lands the canopy at about 340 px/ft, above the 200 floor, without upscaling a whole room.
  2. Assemble on the layers. Place the units on portal-layer-62x25 with the guide PNG as a top layer at 40 percent, the drop on drop-layer-50x25. Keep the opening plum or transparent. Turn the guide layers off before export.
  3. Stack to preview. Drop behind, portal in front, drop offset 6 ft (600 px at working scale). preview-stack.jpg shows the alignment.
  4. Slice. python3 slice_scene.py --portal sweets-portal.png --drop sweets-drop.png --out sweets/ writes the canopy with the opening printed plum and the 1-in cut band, both leg pairs, the drop and a manifest. Add --mask with a black-and-white image of your own silhouette to cut along the illustrated swags instead of the plain arch.
  5. Name and send. Six files per scene, named with piece and size the way Gladsbuy’s own thumbnails are, one Drive link, and a note that they are six distinct images.
Only you can supply

Three decisions

  • The real height of the house black legs. The model now assumes 25 ft; anything up to 27 ft still clears the row-five sightline when flown out.
  • The house measurements that set the zones: first sold row from plaster, its eye height, the widest sold seat.
  • The full-resolution parlor render, so the first real portal layer can be cut from it and checked in the model.
